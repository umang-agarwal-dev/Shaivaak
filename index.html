Local Terrain AI for Smarter Air Quality Forecasts

Global satellite models frequently miscalculate ground-level smog because they miss local hills, valley air pockets, and coastal breezes. We built a localized machine learning model across 20 Indian monitoring stations that eliminates these blind spots — cutting forecast errors by over 31% so cities and citizens receive accurate pollution alerts.
